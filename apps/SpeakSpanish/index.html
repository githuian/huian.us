Speak Spanish

Listen to native pronunciation, then say it out loud. No typing, no writing drills.

Heads up: this browser doesn't support speech recognition, so the "Your turn" scoring won't work here. You can still use "Listen" and practice out loud on your own — try Chrome on desktop or Android for the speaking score.

Today's phrases

Today's set rotates automatically at midnight (your local time) through a bank of spoken phrases across greetings, food, directions, shopping, travel, plans, and everyday small talk.

"Your turn" uses your browser's built-in speech recognition (no server involved on this site) — your browser vendor's speech service may process the audio to transcribe it, same as any other use of your device's dictation feature.
